# Skyward Ballistics privacy policy

_Last updated: November 13, 2026_

Skyward Ballistics is a ballistic calculator for Android and Windows. This policy explains what
the app does with your information. In short: **your data stays on your own device unless you
choose to sync or share it, and we never see it.**

## What we collect

Nothing. Skyward Ballistics has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer
runs no server, and the app never sends your data to the developer or to anyone else on its own.

## What the app keeps on your device

Your firearms, ammo, bullets, target cards, round counts, settings and the last shot you entered
are saved in the app's private storage on your phone or computer. They stay there until you delete
them or uninstall the app.

## Optional features that use the internet or other services

Each of these is off until you turn it on or use it.

- **Android backup.** Android may include your Skyward profiles and display settings in your
  phone's own backup to your Google account. Google runs this, and you control it in your phone's
  settings. Sign-ins and per-device settings are left out of it.
- **Google Drive sync.** If you turn on Drive sync and sign in with Google, Skyward keeps a sync
  file in a hidden app folder in **your own** Google Drive. Skyward can only see that folder, not
  your other Drive files. You can stop syncing and remove access at any time.
- **Reading backups from a Drive folder.** If you choose a Drive folder to read backups from,
  Skyward asks for read-only access to your Drive. It only lists and downloads files from the
  folder you pick, and it never changes, moves or deletes anything in your Drive.
- **Downloading bullet files.** If you enter a web address to download a bullet file, Skyward
  connects only to that address (https only).
- **Component library.** If you choose to download it (Skyward offers it once on first start, and
  under More → Libraries), Skyward downloads Empirical Precision's public component list from
  empiricalprecision.com over https. While "Update automatically" is on, Skyward checks that same
  address about once a week and downloads it again only when it has changed. These are plain
  downloads: nothing about you or your data is sent with them. You can turn updates off or
  remove the library at any time.
- **Location.** If you tap "Use my location" or "Get" for latitude, Skyward asks for your approximate
  location. It uses it only on your device to fill in latitude and compass declination. It is not
  sent anywhere and is not tracked in the background.
- **Profile import from another calculator app (Android).** If you turn on Skyward's profile import
  in Accessibility settings and press Start, Skyward reads the screens of the supported
  calculator app to copy your own firearm, ammo and bullet profiles into Skyward. It only reads
  those apps, only while an import you started is running, and never changes, saves or deletes
  anything in them. The profiles it reads are saved only in Skyward on your phone, and this feature
  makes no network connections.
- **Notifications.** Skyward shows a notification while a profile import is running.
- **Sharing and exporting.** When you export, back up to a folder or share a file, it goes only
  where you choose.

## Windows app

The Windows app saves your data in a folder on your computer and makes no network connections.
If you choose a shared sync file (for example, in a cloud-synced folder), your files go only there.

## Children

Skyward Ballistics is not directed at children.
